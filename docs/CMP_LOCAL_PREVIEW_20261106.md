## Scientific limits and next validation

This is display/engineering verification of existing exploratory evidence. It is not an independent new-outcome experiment or validated improvement to SARIMA point estimates. Calibration remains incomplete, particularly for Cough. Training marginal dispersion is transferred to the SARIMA expected count; this assumption and omitted parameter/model-selection uncertainty remain limitations. Absence of zero-count observations prevents validation of occurrence discrimination. Correct mathematics does not prove reliable future probabilities.

Before operational probability use: freeze the full probability policy and decision criteria before outcomes; archive timestamped predictions using genuinely complete history; use new, subsequently confirmed monthly actuals; compare identical targets against the registered probability baselines; evaluate calibration, Brier/log/RPS, coverage, availability and disease-specific failures, accounting for month/group dependence. Do not treat the inspected October demonstration or overlapping primary/secondary histories as new confirmation. No artificial probability target or calibration tuning was introduced here.
