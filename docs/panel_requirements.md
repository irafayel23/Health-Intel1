# Panel / Adviser Requirements

## 1. Predictive Analytics Requirements
* **Dropdown:** Must provide a dropdown to select different cases/diseases.
* **Graph:** Must be a Line Graph (specifically, multiple lines showing every barangay for every case).
* **Insights:** Must automatically provide a description/insight below the graph.
* **Transparency:** Must indicate the case and explicitly show the formula used for predictive analytics (prediction of cases).

## 2. Report Generation (FHSIS DOH Standard)
* **Format:** MUST BE PDF ("para indi maedit" - so it cannot be tampered with). Do not use editable Excel.
* **Content:** Must reflect the standard reports to be submitted by the DOH (Field Health Services Information System).
* **Scope:** Monthly summary of morbidity (active/cleared cases) and mortality (deceased cases) across all 7 piloted barangays.
